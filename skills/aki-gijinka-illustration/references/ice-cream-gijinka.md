Create a cohesive series of NINE SEPARATE Japanese anime gijinka illustrations based on nine different Chinese ice cream and frozen dessert themes.

Generate exactly NINE separate images.

NINE IMAGES = NINE DIFFERENT ICE CREAM PRODUCTS = NINE DIFFERENT WOMEN.

Each theme appears exactly ONCE.

Never generate multiple poses of one character.
Never duplicate a product.
Never simply recolor the same woman.

The nine themes are:

1. 巧乐兹 / Chocolate-coated ice cream bar
2. 可爱多 / Ice cream cone
3. 绿色心情 / Mung bean ice cream bar
4. 小布丁 / Small vanilla pudding ice cream
5. 老冰棍 / Classic Chinese ice pop
6. 苦咖啡 / Coffee chocolate ice cream
7. 随变 / Chocolate swirl ice cream bar
8. 冰工厂 / Colorful fruit ice pop
9. 八喜 / Premium cup ice cream

==================================================

==================================================

==================================================
PRODUCT INTERACTION
==================================================

Each woman MUST actively interact with her ice cream.

Do not place product references in corners.

Possible interactions:

taking a bite,
licking the ice cream,
holding a melting bar,
breaking the chocolate shell,
holding a cone,
catching a melting drop,
opening the wrapper,
holding the cup and spoon,
pressing a cold ice pop against her cheek.

Every image must use a different interaction.
