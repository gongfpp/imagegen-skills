Create a cohesive series of NINE SEPARATE Japanese anime gijinka illustrations based on nine different Chinese spicy snack / latiao themes.

Generate exactly NINE images.

NINE IMAGES = NINE DIFFERENT PRODUCTS = NINE DIFFERENT WOMEN.

Each product appears exactly ONCE.

Do NOT generate multiple poses of the same character.
Do NOT reuse one character design with different colors.
Do NOT create a collage, grid, contact sheet, lineup, or group portrait.

The nine themes are:

1. 大面筋 / Classic Big Latiao
2. 小面筋 / Mini Latiao
3. 亲嘴烧 / Spicy Kiss Snack
4. 魔芋爽 / Spicy Konjac Snack
5. 素毛肚 / Vegetarian Spicy Tripe
6. 牛筋面 / Spicy Wheat Gluten Noodles
7. 麻辣豆皮 / Spicy Tofu Skin
8. 辣条棒 / Classic Spicy Stick
9. 超辣火药辣条 / Extreme Spicy Latiao

==================================================
GLOBAL ART STYLE
==================================================

adult anime women in their 20s,

Japanese anime fan-art aesthetic,

full-body or nearly full-body illustration,

clean white or very light neutral background,

character occupying 75–85% of canvas,

large expressive anime eyes,

soft adult feminine facial features,

slightly exaggerated but natural anime proportions,

clean but slightly loose hand-drawn lineart,

soft cel shading mixed with subtle painterly brush strokes,

soft skin blush,

glossy detailed hair,

strong anime hair highlights,

slightly imperfect personal illustration feeling,

not commercial advertising art,

not 3D,

not photorealistic.

All nine images must share the SAME rendering style.

However:

CONSISTENT STYLE DOES NOT MEAN SAME CHARACTER.

Every woman must have a different:

product interaction.

Never reuse the same hairstyle.

==================================================
PRODUCT INTERACTION
==================================================

The spicy snack product MUST participate directly in the pose.

Never place the package separately in a corner.

Possible actions include:

tearing open the package,
holding a spicy strip between her lips,
pulling one strip from the bag,
hugging the package,
offering one toward the viewer,
holding several strips like cards,
carrying the package under one arm,
eating directly from the bag,
holding chopsticks with spicy konjac,
or reacting to the heat.

Each image must use a DIFFERENT interaction.
